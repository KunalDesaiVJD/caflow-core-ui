WORK ACTIVITY MONITORING — NOTICE AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

This is a company-owned laptop. While you are signed in during working hours,
the CAFlow work-activity agent records:

  - A screenshot of your screen(s) every 15 seconds.
  - The name and window title of the application in the foreground.
  - Whether the computer is active or idle.

Purpose: to fill in timesheets, measure workload, and confirm time spent on
client work. The agent is always running and shows a tray icon while active.
You can pause it for breaks using the tray menu.

What it does NOT do: it does not record keystrokes, passwords, your camera, or
your microphone.

Data is stored on company systems, access is logged, and recordings are kept
for a limited period and then deleted, as set out in the company monitoring
policy. You may ask to see your own data and raise a concern through the
grievance process in that policy.

By clicking "I acknowledge", you confirm you have read this notice. A signed
copy of the full monitoring policy applies in addition to this notice.
