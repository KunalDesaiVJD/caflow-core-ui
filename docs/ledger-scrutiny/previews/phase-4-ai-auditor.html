Deferred — for your decision

The handoff's Phase 4 also mentions a multi-step agentic investigator — where the AI runs several read-only look-ups in sequence to drill down. I built the grounded single-round version (4.1) first because it's simpler, safer, and — for this data size — nearly as useful.

My recommendation: keep the multi-step loop deferred. It adds real complexity and more AI calls (the gateway caps 20 calls/hour/user), for marginal benefit over the current snapshot approach on a book this size. If you want it, I'll scope it in detail first — say the word.
