Illustrative mock-ups, not live screenshots. Phase 8 is deterministic — no AI, no keys, no migration. It operationalises the engine: turns findings into working-paper evidence, follow-up nudges, a firm worklist and a client feedback loop. Gated behind VITE_SCRUTINY_V2.
🔒 Safety: branch claude/auto-2026-06-21-ledger-scrutiny; nothing pushed to main. Everything here is advisory — nudges, evidence and MIS never block sign-off / UDIN (decision #8); the auditor still confirms every close.

No deploy needed

  • Phase 8 is deterministic — no gateway, keys or migration. It populates once the v2 flag is on.
  • The portal reply path (client answers → Reply Received + client_reply) was already live; 8.6 only adds the auditor-side close.
  • Firm-wide reads (queue, MIS) are RLS-scoped and flag their fetch caps (1,000 findings · 2,000 queries).

What the CA should review

  • SLA thresholds — 2/5d draft review, 7/14d client chase, 3/7d unraised high — right for your practice?
  • Rollover scope — only rp_exist, MSME status/Udyam, MSME-vendor tagging, director loans + RP names carry. Anything else standing enough to carry?
  • Queue lanes — is "low + no query → dispose" the right catch-all, or should low findings also prompt a query?
  • Auto-close — today the client reply suggests a resolve (one click, auditor confirms). Do you ever want it fully automatic?
